# DG5: sfocatura e grana

Prompt effettivo inviato a ImageGen integrato, senza immagini di riferimento. Le due reference utente sono state usate per correggere il profilo testuale prima della generazione.

```text
Create ONE finished 4:5 vertical Italian quote card, Dynamic Gradient style. The quotation is the primary visual message, large and crisp.

STYLE LANGUAGE: A continuous atmospheric colour field built from broad, strongly optically defocused colour concentrations. Geometry is submerged and boundaries are nearly lost. Choose a warm terracotta/apricot pressure toward the upper-left that dissolves gradually into smoky lavender and deep dusty indigo toward the lower-right. This is a palette for this card, not fixed branding. Keep transitions very broad and soft, like colour seen completely out of focus. No recognisable spheres, hard-edged disks, glowing rims, narrow liquid ribbons or 3D objects.

MATERIAL: Dense, fine, visibly tactile dry printed grain covers the entire colour field. The masses have strong low-frequency blur while their high-frequency microtexture remains crisp. Grain must be perceptible at card scale, including both lighter and darker areas. Use a fine natural paper/dither tooth, integrated into the colour; no big dirt specks, scratches, stains, rigid checker pattern or literal fabric photographed as a product. The finish is matte, softly atmospheric and materially rich.

COMPOSITION: Use unequal broad colour masses to make a gently diagonal field. Give the main writing zone a continuous darker, sufficiently even local tone so every white letter stays legible. Keep the gradient integrated throughout, with no box or panel behind the quote. Place the type as one substantial left-aligned block slightly above the vertical centre, with balanced margins and natural line spacing. The widest line should occupy roughly three quarters of the usable width; text must be the first thing read, not a small caption in a large empty background.

TEXT: Render exactly once, in a single clean contemporary geometric/grotesque sans-serif of regular-to-medium weight, bright off-white, with sharp edges and natural glyph proportions and word spacing. Use these three lines:
Ogni scelta
dà forma
al futuro.

Preserve the grave accent in dà and the final period. Quotation marks delimiting this instruction are not part of the card. No attribution, signature, extra copy, heading, logo, label, icon or mockup. Do not blur the letters or let the grain break their strokes. Deliver the full-bleed flat card only.
```
