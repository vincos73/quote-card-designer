# Prompt dell’esempio

Composizione dimostrativa: il soggetto e le parole appartengono a questa card.
Non usare questo prompt come impaginato fisso per nuove citazioni.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished artistic social quote card.
Generate a new image from the following textual style profile. The profile itself is not text to place in the artwork.

Design Style:
  Name: "Neon Collage"
  Concept: >
    High-stimulus editorial design informed by digital marketing, Y2K internet,
    vaporwave, risograph printing, street posters and social carousels. Recompose
    digital information overload into graphic rhythm that catches attention
    within noise. Re-edit monochrome cutouts of sculptures, animals, people and
    everyday technology with fluorescent colour and playful juxtaposition.
  Canvas:
    Ratio: "4:5"
    Output: "One flat social quote card"
  Mood:
    - Bold
    - Energetic
    - Playful
    - Internet-native
    - Experimental
    - Rebellious
    - Graphic
    - Attention-grabbing
  Temperature:
    Overall: "Hyper-saturated"
  Color Palette:
    Background:
      ElectricBlue: "#0048FF"
      NeonPink: "#FF3CCF"
      AcidLime: "#D8FF2E"
    Primary:
      ElectricBlue: "#0048FF"
    Secondary:
      NeonPink: "#FF3CCF"
      AcidLime: "#D8FF2E"
    Accent:
      White: "#FFFFFF"
      Black: "#111111"
    Roles: >
      Electric blue establishes the main field. Pink and lime interrupt it as
      decisive planes and message strips. Black and white carry photographic
      cutouts, contrast and legible type. Restrict this card to these five colours.
  Typography:
    Headlines:
      Style: >
        Extra-bold condensed sans-serif, huge and tightly fitted. Treat type as
        structural graphic material. A few oversized forms may push against
        boundaries, while the complete quotation remains readable.
      Weight: "800-900"
      Tracking: "Tight"
      Case: "Mixed; preserve the supplied quotation"
      Scale: "Oversized"
    Secondary:
      Style: "Poster sans-serif; fluorescent-label treatment for emphasis"
      Weight: "500-700"
    Body:
      Style: "Simple sans-serif; only for supplied supporting content"
      Weight: 400
      LineHeight: "1.2-1.4"
  Layout:
    Structure: >
      Dense editorial composition combining a social carousel cover and a street
      poster. A regular underlying grid supports deliberate trespass by type,
      cutouts and label strips. Maintain hierarchy through scale and contrast.
    Composition:
      - "Oversized structural typography"
      - "Collage layering"
      - "Dynamic asymmetry"
      - "Cutout imagery and floating message strips"
      - "Visual interruptions and aggressive overlap"
    Alignment:
      Preference: "Grid with disruption"
      ControlledChaos: "Medium"
  Graphic Elements:
    Shapes:
      - "Highlight strips and tape-like elements"
      - "Rounded labels and rectangular panels"
      - "Irregular cutouts and abstract blobs"
      - "Thick crosses and circles"
    Symbols:
      - "TV test-pattern fragments"
      - "Interface-like shapes without invented UI text"
      - "Arrows, dots, warning marks and smile icons"
      - "Graphic crosses and decorative underlines"
    Decorative Rules:
      - "Allow secondary marks to compete for attention."
      - "Use repetition to create rhythm."
      - "Overlap assertively and preserve hierarchy through scale."
      - "Select compatible devices rather than including the entire repertoire."
    Patterns:
      - "Halftone"
      - "Risograph grain and offset print texture"
      - "Mild print misregistration and digital noise"
      - "Typographic repetition only when the supplied copy permits it"
  Imagery:
    Style: >
      Monochrome photographic cutouts and classical imagery boldly re-edited with
      neon colour. Treat the selected subject as a playful graphic protagonist,
      integrated into the typography and planes.
    Treatment:
      - "Black-and-white cutouts"
      - "Thick coloured outlines"
      - "Duotone accents"
      - "Posterized contrast"
      - "Mixed scales and layered integration"
      - "Minimal shadows"
    Subjects:
      - "Classical sculptures and animals"
      - "Portraits and human gestures"
      - "Vintage electronics and everyday objects"
      - "Internet-culture motifs"
  Background:
    Style: >
      Switch boldly between blue, pink and lime planes. Use enlarged typography,
      pattern and graphic interruptions to evoke dense digital space, without
      inventing extra words to simulate information.
  Effects:
    Texture: "Risograph grain, offset print, slight halftone and digital noise"
    Shadow: "Minimal"
    Depth: "Flat collage layering"
    Distortion: "Mild print misregistration, occasional scan effects and controlled glitches"
  Visual Rules:
    - "Use blue, pink, lime, black and white only."
    - "Use oversized typography as a structural element."
    - "Apply fluorescent highlight strips to key messages."
    - "Allow text to overlap imagery without obscuring words."
    - "Create surprise and humour through collage."
    - "Balance visual chaos with clear focal points."
    - "Preserve mobile readability and feed stopping power."
  Exclusions:
    - "Corporate minimalism and overly clean layouts"
    - "Luxury aesthetics and elegant serif typography"
    - "Muted earth tones and soft watercolour"
    - "Glassmorphism"
    - "Photorealistic lifestyle scenes"
    - "Complex 3D rendering"

Card-specific concept:
A chunky retro CRT television becomes an improbable label printer. A large black-and-white photographic cutout hand pulls a fluorescent lime strip from its screen; that strip carries the final clause "si progetta." Treat the impossible action with playful visual wit. The television is a posterized photographic cutout with a thick neon-pink outline, not a CGI product render. Let a fragment of blue/pink/lime test pattern and coarse halftone give the screen a printed digital texture. Use this one central collage relationship, not a collection of every subject in the repertoire.

Composition:
Build the reading sequence through the actual quotation. "Il futuro" is oversized condensed structural typography. "non si aspetta:" is a smaller but clearly readable contrasting strip. "si progetta." is the forceful final lime message pulled from the screen. Reflow the words as needed for the portrait canvas while preserving their order. Let cutouts, label edges and text overlap assertively, and vary their scales. The image and words occupy one interlocking poster composition, rather than a title over a separate photograph. Include only a few related secondary marks such as thick crosses, arrows or repeated dots to create rhythm around the central action. No sterile empty frame, no gradient glow: obtain the neon impact from saturated flat colour.

Text (verbatim), exactly once:
Il futuro non si aspetta: si progetta.

Accuracy:
Preserve every word, capitalization and punctuation, including the colon and final period. Do not crop, cover or distort any letter enough to make it ambiguous. No author is available. No additional labels, captions, interface text, dates, brands, digits, logos, random background lettering or watermarks. Use graphic noise rather than invented text. No other phrases and no duplicate quotation. Deliver only the single flat card, not a mockup or a style board.
```
