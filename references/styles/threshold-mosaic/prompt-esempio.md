> Create one finished editorial quote card, portrait 4:5, in the Threshold Mosaic visual grammar. Use the three supplied images only as structural references for a granular transition from scattered square microcells to a dense fine mesh and finally a substantial continuous color plane with irregular stepped boundary. Do not copy their palette, exact contour, arrangement, or landscape format. CRITICAL SCALE: microcells must be far smaller than in a typical pixel art poster, each no more than about one fifteenth of the quote capital height, with hundreds of tiny crisp squares across the active field. Small even square pitch throughout; density changes by cell occupancy, NEVER by increasing square size. At thumbnail size the cells should read as granular texture, while at full size they remain individually square. Composition: a copper color mass rises diagonally from the lower left into dark aubergine negative space; its upper edge transitions through the tiny square mesh, and the mesh travels near but never through the text. Use the palette independently of the references: deep aubergine #231521 background, muted copper #C8754D plane, ivory #F7F2E7 quote, few sage #A1B9A5 accent microcells. Large, clean, geometric sans serif quote on calm dark ground, clearly the dominant first read, natural letterforms and line breaks. Exact Italian text ONCE: 'Ogni scelta dà forma al futuro.' Preserve case, accent, spaces and final period. No quote marks, attribution, labels, logo, other words or figures. Flat matte print, crisp edges, restrained tactile grain. Avoid medium or large squares, chunky checkerboard, blurry dithering, gradients, UI, 3D, and any symbols larger than the tiny cells.
