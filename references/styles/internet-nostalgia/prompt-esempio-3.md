Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one complete vertical 4:5 artistic quotation card, full bleed, flat digital artwork, not a poster photograph or record-cover mockup.

Create an original Y2K cyber-futurist quotation artwork. The visual language is optimistic turn-of-millennium digital futurism: liquid polished silver chrome, holographic optical diffraction, softly inflated smooth forms, translucent candy plastics, and small floating computer-interface fragments. Design the entire card, including the wording and materials, together.

The ONLY written content, verbatim and exactly once, is:
"IL FUTURO HA BISOGNO DI SOGNI."
Suggested reading groups:
IL FUTURO
HA BISOGNO
DI SOGNI.
No attribution or signature.

Make the sentence a large main visual form occupying the upper half and extending into the middle of the composition. Use one coherent bold rounded futuristic Latin grotesk, slightly forward-leaning, with substantial clean counters, natural proportions and distinct word spaces. Give the letter surfaces a bright SILVER CHROME finish, thick readable dark-indigo contour and controlled sculptural bevels. Reflect cobalt, cyan and lilac on the metal while retaining a very clear silhouette and legible dark boundaries. The text should look cast from reflective material, not flat black type on a blank paper panel. All words, including IL, HA and DI, remain substantial and readable at phone scale. Preserve every letter, word space and the final period. Do not melt away the reading forms, squeeze a single word or scatter the sentence into tiny floating labels.

In the lower half, compose ONE large unbranded iridescent optical disc, seen at a pronounced diagonal, interlocked with a smooth organic liquid-chrome form. The disc has a believable central hole, thin reflective edge and directional rainbow diffraction; not a flat rainbow sticker. The chrome form bends around part of the disc and echoes a curve in the text, building one continuous metallic visual conversation. Keep it clear of required letters. Two much smaller reflective droplets can establish depth and balance without filling every gap.

Background: softly shaded cool silver with an asymmetrical luminous cobalt-to-lilac elliptical haze behind the typography and disc. It is a synthetic digital space, with a restrained fine grid or orbital contour in selected areas. A small pair of translucent floating interface tiles can show a simple wireframe globe and a graphic progress bar, respectively. Their thin window edges and rounded transparent surfaces belong to the same cool material system; they are quiet secondary details with no words, numbers, codes or file names.

Palette for this study: silver and cool gray, cobalt #3155ED, pale cyan #A2F0FF, lilac #B8A4F7 and dark ink #17183C for letter definition. Purposeful brilliant highlights, controlled lens bloom and clear local contrast. A few optical light points may enrich the metal, but no repeated four-point star stamps in the corners. Digital polish and smooth manufactured materials; no antique paper, torn collage, heavy grain or grunge.

The quote must remain the first reading, with strong safe margins and original visual relationships. Do not put it in an oversized ivory rectangle surrounded by a stock sticker border. No compulsory pixel font, rainbow arch, kittens, flowers or vintage-web icon inventory. No brand logos, product labels, celebrity likenesses, random Asian characters, barcodes, serial numbers, invented microtext, headings such as Y2K or CHROME, watermarks or additional prose.
