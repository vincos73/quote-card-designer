    Roles: Ivory and low-saturation porcelain blue define the page. All landscape, botanical and
      architectural imagery uses tonal values of one blue ink. Dark blue carries the quotation, never
      pure black. No warm-coloured accents or vivid primary colours; warmth belongs only to the ivory
      paper.
  Typography:
    Primary:
      Style: Tall high-contrast classical serif with restrained decorative elegance
      Weight: Regular to medium, with legible fine strokes
      Role: Large quotation set as one coherent typographic voice
    Supporting:
      Style: Readable classical serif, optional short italic passage when explicitly appropriate
      Role: Only supplied supporting copy or attribution
    Hierarchy: A clear large quotation in a quiet pale field. Use natural line breaks and one consistent
      type size and glyph construction. Decorative character comes from the serif shapes, not illegible
      flourishes, multiple font changes or a long script passage. Preserve sentence capitalization.
  Layout:
    Structure:
    - A luminous central reading field
    - Balanced or subtly symmetrical cover-like composition
    - Organic illustration framing at the edges and lower field
    - Generous mist, sky and ivory negative space
    Relationships: Let branches, landscape contours and pale atmospheric layers create an open natural
      frame around the quotation. Keep dark foreground details near the margins; distant forms become
      pale and dissolve into the reading field. Avoid a dense vignette directly behind the words.
      Central alignment is a useful first direction but not a mandatory template for every quotation.
  Imagery:
    Style: Monochrome-blue watercolour, delicate engraved botanical detail and porcelain-like illustration
    Subjects:
    - Misty mountains and waterside landscapes
    - Trees, flowering branches and botanical studies
    - Birds, small animals and cloud forms
    - Gardens, distant architecture and symbolic objects
    Selection: Choose a coherent small illustration group relevant to the words. One to three framing
      concentrations are sufficient; do not catalogue every motif. Architecture is optional, not
      a compulsory cultural identifier.
    Treatment:
    - Softly bleeding outlines and matte pigment
    - Darker detailed foreground plants and pale distant landscape
    - Air perspective and luminous open mist
    - Fine reproduction grain rather than photographic realism
    Avoid:
    - Full-colour photography
    - Glossy porcelain product mockups
    - Flat cartoon scenery, anime figures and 3D rendering
  Graphic Elements:
    Repertoire:
    - Fine blue-grey book-like rules when needed
    - A delicate botanical or landscape boundary
    - An optional quiet ivory reading opening continuous with the paper
    Restraint: For a short quotation prefer mist and pale sky to opaque panels. Do not import presentation
      cards, translucent UI widgets or a stack of content boxes. Botanical elements should frame
      rather than crowd the words.
  Background:
    Surface: Warm ivory antique paper, subtle fibre and pale blue watercolour
    Depth: Atmospheric tonal distance within the illustration
    Reading Field: Central paper stays pale and quiet, with no busy detail under letters
  Effects:
    Texture: Delicate paper grain and fine printed pigment
    Bleed: Soft watercolour edges, controlled and sparse near text
    Shadows: None
    Gradients: Only natural tonal wash and atmospheric fading, no digital colour-gradient effects
    Finish: Matte illustrated page

  - Use only supplied attribution and supporting text.
  - Do not invent Chinese characters, seals with illegible text, captions, signatures, dates or decorative
