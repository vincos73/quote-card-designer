# Ballpen — prova blu

La card corrente deriva da una generazione completa seguita da una correzione
mirata delle lettere I. L'unica immagine passata al secondo prompt è la prima
bozza nativa, conservata come [bozza-blu-prima-correzione.png](bozza-blu-prima-correzione.png).
L'immagine dell'utente è stata osservata per definire la differenza visiva, ma
non è stata passata a ImageGen.

## Prompt iniziale effettivo

```text
Create ONE complete vertical 4:5 Italian quote card, shown as the flat scanned artwork itself on uncoated cream paper. This is a BLUE-MONO Ballpen artwork: all visible ink is physically plausible cobalt-blue oil-paste ballpoint pen ink, with darker blue only where strokes layer. No other pigment, no black lettering.

The penwork is restless, spontaneous, visibly chaotic and authentically handmade: variable pressure, fine scratchy lines, occasional ink skips, doubled contours, dense hatching and crosshatching, tiny dark pools where strokes begin and end, several small ink-transfer smears and stains, spontaneous loops and false starts, and partly crossed-out compass construction lines. Concentrate the disorder in a few intentional high-density clusters rather than applying a uniform texture. Show dry dragged ballpoint smudges and pressure changes, never watery washes or brush-shaped marks.

The quotation is the only readable text anywhere. Draw the lettering itself by hand using the same blue ballpoint pen, ALL CAPS, with naturally differing letter widths and a little irregular slant and spacing. It must not resemble a clean digital font. Render these exact three lines ONCE, preserving every letter, word space, punctuation mark, and reading order:
ANCHE IL CAOS
HA UNA
DIREZIONE.

Make the quotation large and clearly legible at phone size in the upper 45% of the page. Maintain a light irregular halo of clearer paper immediately around the letter cores and counters. Handwritten character and irregular spacing should convey disorder without altering or obscuring the words. No attribution, no additional words, no fake writing, labels, dates, signatures, logos, or microtext.

In the lower 45%, draw a large rough hand-drawn compass rose, off-center toward the lower right and partially cropped by the right and bottom edges. It is recognizable as a compass rose through its radial geometry, long directional point, and directional arrow, but it has NO letters or numeric labels. Add tangled route-like spirals, repeated exploratory lines, abandoned construction circles, crossed-out geometric attempts, and agitated marginal scribbles. Some strokes echo around the quotation while keeping all letter counters and the reading path clear. The text and drawing belong to the same restless sheet of penwork.

Use only realistic cobalt blue and layered darker blue on cream paper. Preserve the fine oily line quality of a ballpoint pen. No paint, watercolor, brush, marker, pencil, sprayed or digital grunge overlay, photographic desk scene, physical pen, notebook binding, mockup, frame, or contact sheet. The result is one finished flat scanned quote card.
```

## Prompt di correzione effettivo

```text
Make ONE targeted typography correction to this supplied blue-ballpoint quote card, preserving its existing composition, cream paper, chaotic cobalt-blue oil-paste ballpoint penwork, compass rose, directional arrow, crossed-out construction lines, route-like spirals, localized ink-transfer smudges, stains and small blots.

The exact quotation must be entirely in UPPERCASE, appearing once:
ANCHE IL CAOS
HA UNA
DIREZIONE.

Correct every dotted lowercase i in the existing words to a proper undotted UPPERCASE I. In particular, the I in IL and both I characters in DIREZIONE must have no dot above them. They must be clearly hand-drawn uppercase I strokes, matching the restless blue ballpoint lettering. Do not add a new dot, retain a stray dot as a diacritic, or substitute a lowercase l. Preserve every other letter, the word spaces, the final period, the three-line reading order and the large phone-legible lettering. Maintain the original handmade irregular widths, line hatching and character; do not replace the lettering with a digital typeface.

Keep the lower-right compass illustration and all other geometry and paper as close to the supplied artwork as possible. Preserve obvious localized authentic ballpoint smears, pressure changes and ink blots. No elegant cursive lettering or calligraphic swooshes, no neat little landscape vignette, no plant or road scene. No new readable text, compass labels, dates, numbers, signature, attribution or logos. All ink remains blue, with darker blue only from layering. No other pigment, no black text, paint, watercolor, brush, marker, pencil or digital grunge overlay. Keep the existing flat vertical format.
```
