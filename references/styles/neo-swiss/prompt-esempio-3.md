# Neo Swiss · prova 3, asse spostato

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE complete flat Italian editorial quote card, portrait 4:5, full bleed.

PRIMARY REQUEST: A new Neo Swiss study with a clear flexible grid, confident contemporary grotesk typography, generous active whitespace and one deliberate displacement. Compose the entire card, both lettering and graphic form. No reference images are supplied; this is a test of the written design profile.

PALETTE AND MATERIAL: Warm near-white uncoated paper #F6F4EE, deep black ink #141414, one flat vermilion spot ink #E6462D. Very subtle print texture only. Sharp flat ink boundaries. No gradients, shadows, bevels, glass, distress or photographed mockup.

COMPOSITION: Two substantial left-aligned quotation groups share the same invisible modular system. The first group establishes an upper-left reading axis. The second group is lower and shifted right by one generous module; the eye must move naturally from the first to the second. Give their separation an intentional open interval. Use only ONE graphic event: a broad flat vermilion boundary near the right part of the page that makes a single outward step at the interval between the quotation groups, continuing or cropping at the page edge. Its displacement echoes the displacement of the reading axis. It is a bold colour-plane edge, not a technical diagram, thin construction grid, frame or arrow. Text and the stepped plane share the same page and their spacing is deliberate.

The graphic step should express the idea of a boundary being moved, without literally illustrating a wall or introducing a symbol. Leave the grid itself unprinted. Avoid any collection of decorative rules, circles, registration marks, nodes or small labels.

TEXT — render this exact quotation ONCE, including the opening and closing guillemets and final period:
«Una buona domanda sposta il confine.»

Arrange it in these four lines and two semantic groups:
«Una buona
domanda

sposta
il confine.»

TYPOGRAPHY: Clear contemporary Swiss grotesk sans serif, visually in the broad direction of Akzidenz-Grotesk or Univers, medium-to-bold with open counters. Use ONE coherent size and weight across all four lines, at a substantial editorial scale. Letters have natural widths, carefully judged kerning and visible ordinary word spaces. Do not compress or stretch any word. The first quotation group is large and upper-left; the second keeps the same natural proportions on its shifted axis. Compact but clearly open leading. All baselines horizontal. The guillemets may hang optically into the margins but stay complete. Every glyph and punctuation mark must be intact and immediately readable. Keep the vermilion form in a supporting relationship and protect strong local contrast around the words.

CONTENT CONSTRAINTS: No author or signature. No extra words, title, repeated giant initial, numbers, dates, logos, captions, microtext, exhibition credits or decorative pseudo-data. No cropped letters, touching glyphs, chaotic word placement, font changes per word or exaggerated black weight. Deliver one resolved contemporary editorial poster-card, not a grid of alternatives.
```
