Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one finished flat editorial quote card, portrait 4:5, ideally 1280 x 1600 pixels. The entire image is the artwork; no photograph of a sheet, no mockup, no frame or shadow.

Primary request: Compose both the complete typography and the graphic construction of a contemporary Neo Swiss poster. Use a lightly exposed modular grid, confident neo-grotesque typography, strong but disciplined contrasts of scale, asymmetric balance and active empty modules. Precise, lucid, rhythmic and visually arresting.

Composition: A few shared vertical column edges and horizontal bands organize the page. The opening group establishes a clear left edge in the upper part. The main word 'domanda' is the dominant, substantially larger typographic anchor. The closing group continues below, shifted by one real grid interval; the full reading order remains unmistakably top to bottom. A single vermilion horizontal plane extends across a module boundary in the lower portion, giving visual form to the idea of moving a boundary. Integrate it with the text and open space rather than placing an illustration under a heading. Keep some modules entirely empty. Keep generous but deliberate asymmetric spacing. Compose the phrase into semantic line groups; do not isolate function words. Do not force equal line widths.

Typography: A clear, naturally proportioned Helvetica/Univers-like neo-grotesque, with optical kerning, open counters, unambiguous word spaces and intact punctuation. Regular or medium opening and closing groups contrast with the larger bold 'domanda', all belonging to one coherent family. No artificial condensation, stretching, negative tracking to fill a box, or uniform black-weight wall of text. All quotation letters and both guillemets must be complete and inside the canvas.

Visible construction: Reveal a restrained selection of the actual module boundaries with extremely fine pale vermilion hairlines. They must be gently discernible on the full-size artwork, clearly secondary to the quotation, approximately the visual effect of 1-pixel rules in a diluted 12-percent spot-colour ink at this scale. Align the text edges and colour plane with the very same grid. Reveal the structure through margins and empty modules. Interrupt the rules around letterforms where needed: no line striking through words, no individually boxed words, no dense graph paper, no measurement ticks, nodes, corner crop marks, interface controls or invented annotations.

Palette and surface: Warm pale ivory paper, near-black reading ink, one flat vermilion accent. Colour is a compositional plane rather than a highlighter behind arbitrary words. Flat clear print, nearly smooth paper, no grain that damages the letters, no gradients, bevels, gloss or 3D effects.

Text, verbatim, exactly once:
«Una buona domanda sposta il confine.»

Preserve this exact wording, capitalization and punctuation, including opening « and closing », with the period before the closing guillemet. Line breaks are free. No author, personal signature, title, caption, numbers, dates, background words, repeated text or other lettering. No reference images are supplied: this is a test of the written style profile, not a reproduction of an existing poster.
