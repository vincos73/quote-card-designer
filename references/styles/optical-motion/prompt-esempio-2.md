Create one finished flat Optical / Op Art quote card, vertical 4:5. Deep black and warm ivory only, sharp printed graphic edges. No photography, 3D rendering, shadows, blur or glossy surface.

Exact and only text, once, preserving case, accent and punctuation:
Ogni punto di vista apre una possibilità.
No quotation marks, attribution, title or signature.

Build a single regular field of closely repeated straight vertical black and ivory stripes. Let four substantial lines of bold contemporary grotesk lettering emerge from a controlled inversion of that field:

The striped pattern continues through the large letter silhouettes, but its black/ivory phase reverses inside them, so the words seem to move in and out of registration with the background. Keep the outer shapes and counters of every letter decisive and immediately readable. Where needed, preserve a very fine continuous letter boundary to clarify the reading without turning the words into solid caption boxes. Use fewer, wider stripes than a microscopic screen pattern; the lettering must remain legible at phone size. Text dominates the middle of the page, with natural word spaces and clean line separation. The stripes stay straight and parallel: the illusion comes from phase and figure-ground reversal, not from wavy bubble outlines, repeating the phrase, offset shadows or a separate decorative tunnel. A restrained, precise perceptual experiment in which the words inhabit the pattern itself.
