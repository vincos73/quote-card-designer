# Marker Editorial — variante fotografica

Stessa frase dimostrativa originale: **LASCIA IL SEGNO**, senza punteggiatura finale.
Ritratto immaginario generato insieme alla composizione; nessuna fotografia reale fornita come target.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: second editorial quote-card style study, vertical 4:5, flat finished artwork edge to edge.
Primary request: Extend a crisp-typography plus physical marker style into a photographic editorial collage. A black-and-white photographic portrait is visibly scribbled over by broad black felt pen and fluorescent yellow highlighter, while clean printed words remain the dominant message.
The ONLY text is exactly once:
LASCIA
IL SEGNO
No period, quotation marks, author, title, logo, number, signature or extra writing.
Use a newly imagined anonymous adult person, not a celebrity. Photorealistic candid monochrome three-quarter portrait from chest up, natural skin and hair texture, believable anatomy, simple dark textured jacket, assertive relaxed expression. A large photographic fragment occupies the middle and upper-right of a white paper composition, freely cropped by the right edge, with the face above the center. The photographic interior retains continuous grayscale detail; no comic conversion, screenprint coloring or full-body sticker outline.
Typography is printed very bold black Swiss grotesk sans serif, natural glyph proportions and optical spacing, not marker handwriting. LASCIA occupies the upper-left negative space and overlaps only a clear light peripheral part of the photographic crop. IL SEGNO runs in one powerful horizontal line across the lower central white field, slightly crossing the bottom of the jacket while preserving every glyph. The text reads first and in order. Leave clear margins around the full words.
Marker intervention: a broad fluorescent yellow loose loop sweeps around the outside of the head and bends into the surrounding paper, with a few translucent passes across hair and jacket. Add rough, uneven black marker scribble strokes over part of the jacket and the empty surrounding field, three quick gestural slashes and an energetic open black loop responding to the shoulder. Keep eyes, nose and mouth clearly visible; preserve the recognizable portrait. One or two black hand-drawn arrows from open paper direct the gaze toward the words, avoiding letter collisions. A loose yellow ellipse partly encircles IL SEGNO. The marks extend across the photographic edge onto paper so photograph, type and marks share one surface. No tidy decoration confined to a border.
Black ink has dry chisel-tip ridges, overlaps and small streaks. Yellow highlighter is acid-bright, translucent and layered, not a neon glow. White matte lightly fibrous paper. Limit visual color to black, grayscale, white and fluorescent yellow. Allow white breathing space and unequal mark density.
Avoid a title above a rectangular photo, a neutral photo with a pasted caption strip, handwritten quote lettering, paintbrush texture, cartoon portrait, repeated words, logos, symbols or additional text. Original finished poster, no frame or mockup.
```
