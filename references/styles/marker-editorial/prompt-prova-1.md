# Marker Editorial — prova tipografica

Frase dimostrativa originale, senza attribuzione: **LASCIA IL SEGNO**.
Generazione da descrizione; allegato osservato e tradotto in indicazioni, non passato al generatore.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original editorial quote-card style study, vertical 4:5, flat finished artwork edge to edge.
Primary request: Create a confident marker-intervention typographic poster. Crisp printed typography is deliberately attacked and emphasized by tactile black felt-tip arrows and fluorescent yellow highlighter gestures. White uncoated paper, contemporary graphic design, physical marker material.
The ONLY text is exactly once:
LASCIA
IL SEGNO
No period, quotation marks, author, title, logo, serial number, signature or extra writing. Preserve all letters and the natural space between IL and SEGNO.
Typography: very large black heavyweight Swiss grotesk sans serif, clean natural glyph proportions, open counters, tight but optical spacing. Printed type, not handwritten lettering. The two semantic lines occupy the main central field, broadly left aligned. LASCIA is slightly smaller than IL SEGNO. The words dominate instantly and remain completely legible.
Composition: an energetic page with white intervals, not a centered plaque. Across the upper and lower outer fields, loose large fluorescent acid-yellow marker arcs and diagonal gestures run out of frame. A large double freehand yellow oval circles IL SEGNO, with one translucent sweep crossing only the lower portion of the black letters so they remain readable. Three or four large black hand-drawn arrows approach the words from different page edges; their unequal shafts and bold angled arrowheads visibly show broad chisel-tip felt pen pressure, dry ridges and streaking. Keep arrowheads clear of letters and word spacing. A short heavy black marker scrub sits in an upper peripheral space, separate from every word, as a material accent; no erased duplicate words or concealed extra text.
Materials: black marker is matte and slightly uneven, with overlaps, dense ink and a few dry fibers along edges. Fluorescent yellow is saturated, translucent and visibly layered where passes cross. Soft fine white paper grain, without grime or vintage damage. Clean vector-like printed type contrasts with imperfect hand-made gestures.
Avoid a repeated arrow pattern, symmetrical radial sunburst, doodle icons, stars, framing border, gradients, glow, 3D, drop shadows, red paint, brush calligraphy or a photographed desk. Only black, fluorescent yellow and white. Create an original arrangement, not a facsimile of a reference poster.
```
