ONLY text, exactly as written, lowercase, no period or extra punctuation:
le idee
non nascono
perfette

Real comfortable margins on both sides. Dots of the i stay intact and recognizably part of their letters.
Composition and meaning: around the type, marks suggest thinking and revision in progress. In the upper white field, one small dense black chisel-marker scribble with overlapping strokes, beside an incomplete rough oval trial. Physical absorption into lightly textured bright white paper, no neon glow, shadow or dimensional paint.
No photograph in this version, no box around the quote, no duplicate text as erasures, no decorative icons, no stationery, no mockup, no frame, no background grunge or vintage damage. Deliver a highly legible original 4:5 graphic print.
