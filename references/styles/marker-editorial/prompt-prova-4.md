```text
Edit the supplied quote poster. Keep the canvas 4:5, white paper, black printed typography, exact phrase, all five words, lowercase, three existing line breaks, glyph positions, sizes and proportions unchanged. The ONLY text remains exactly:
le idee

perfette
No added period, extra writing, signature, numbers or labels.

Revise only the drawn marker interventions so the poster feels physically marked by a person thinking on paper, more spontaneous and realistic, less polished or designed. Preserve the existing general division between open upper field and large lower quotation.

1. REMOVE the oversized black arrow from the upper-right field. In its place draw a medium-small original unfinished LIGHT BULB doodle with a broad black felt-tip marker, roughly within the same open upper-right area. This is a working idea sketched in a notebook, not an icon: a lopsided open bulb contour built from two imperfectly meeting strokes, a tiny crooked filament, a base suggested with two or three short uneven horizontal passes, and three unequal quick rays. A slight correction/retraced contour must remain visible. Use confident but imperfect marks with changes in tip angle and pressure. Keep it secondary to the text. No words inside or near it.
2. In the upper-left area replace the orderly parallel black scratch patch and smooth oval with a looser scribbled tangle and a partial attempted loop: irregular overlapping strokes, a couple of thin scratch lines, one ink-heavy stopping point, a small dry patch. It should look like a real exploratory pen gesture, not an ornamental abstract emblem. Leave paper between the marks.
3. Make the fluorescent yellow-green highlighter marks convincingly hand-made: lighter translucent passes, width variation, a slightly abrupt turn on the upper sweeping stroke, visible overlap darkening and uneven starts and stops. Around perfette use a hasty wonky oval, two nonparallel passes that wander apart and remain open, with one slightly overshooting tail. Do not make a geometric ellipse. Keep every word and every letter readable, including all descenders and the dot on the i.
4. The black lower curve becomes a more incidental short unfinished swoosh with a small hesitation, not a perfect smile.

Physical material is crucial: dense matte black alcohol/felt marker on uncoated white paper, absorbed ink, subtly feathered contact edges, pressure changes, small ink pooling where the marker pauses, occasional pale stroke core where it dries. Smooth felt tip rather than shaggy paintbrush bristles. Fluorescent yellow-green highlighter is translucent and flat, with no glow. Keep the paper light and clean with subtle natural tooth. Do not add staged photographic lighting, shadows, old-paper grime or artificial texture over everything.

The intention is the formation of an imperfect idea expressed through actual informal doodles. Avoid tidy stock lightbulb iconography, vector geometry, decorative star collections, little handwritten text, symmetrical arrows or more drawings across the words. Maintain generous breathing space and the original dominant typography.
```
