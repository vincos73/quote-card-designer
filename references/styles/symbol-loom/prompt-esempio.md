# Prompt effettivo, esempio 1

Input visivi passati a ImageGen: `reference-1.png`, `reference-2.png`. Strumento: ImageGen integrato.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE finished 4:5 editorial quote card for the experimental style Symbol Loom. The two input images are references for the behavior of a modular symbol alphabet only: many tiny repeated X marks, target circles in square cells, solid squares and diamonds; locally changing runs, gaps and density. Create a NEW composition, never copy their exact silhouette, columns, colors or symbol sequence. The pattern MUST be made of many small cells, approximately 30 to 45 modules across the active card width; each mark is at most one quarter to one third of the quote's capital height. No large isolated X, target or diamond. A broad forked field of these small signs diverges around the quote, but streams remain woven alongside part of the text rather than becoming separate strips. Text is the dominant first read, very large clean geometric sans serif, natural letterforms, 2 or 3 intentional lines, deep plum #2D193F on quiet pale lavender paper #F4EDF2. Pattern ink periwinkle #6F70E8 with restrained tangerine #F08C65; these colors are chosen independently of the reference images. Print is flat, crisp, matte, slight paper tactility. Exact visible text ONCE, case and accent exact: 'Ogni scelta dà forma al futuro.' No quotation marks, no other text, attribution, signature, logos or filler. Keep all words intact, entirely legible, no symbols touching glyphs. Avoid pixel halftone, decorative confetti, game board, gradients, 3D.
