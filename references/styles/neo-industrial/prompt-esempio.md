# Prompt dell’esempio

Composizione dimostrativa: il soggetto e le parole appartengono a questa card.
Non usare questo prompt come impaginato fisso per nuove citazioni.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished artistic social quote card.
Create the card using the following structured design style. The profile is art direction, not lettering to reproduce in the image.

Design Style:
  Name: "Neo Industrial Editorial"
  Concept: >
    Contemporary editorial graphics informed by technical manuals, industrial
    visual systems and experimental typography. Modular construction, disciplined
    type, engineered diagrams and patterned ink surfaces form one coherent visual
    language. Precision, shifts of scale and the relationship between open
    construction and solid form provide the expressive character.

  Canvas:
    Ratio: "4:5"
    Output: "Single flat social quote card, full bleed"

  Mood:
    - Engineered
    - Editorial
    - Experimental
    - Precise
    - Tactile
    - Assertive

  Color Palette:
    Background:
      Primary: "#F0EEE6"
    Text:
      Primary: "#202321"
      Inverse: "#F0EEE6"
    Accent:
      Signal Orange: "#FF5C1A"
      Technical Grey: "#96988F"
    Roles: >
      Paper and dark ink carry the large masses. Orange identifies a structural
      transition or focal relationship. Grey supports the fine construction layer.
      Use these roles intentionally rather than colouring arbitrary words.

  Typography:
    Primary:
      Style: "Bold industrial grotesk; proportional, closely fitted, optically balanced"
      Weight: 700
      Role: "Main quotation and its strongest clause"
    Secondary:
      Style: "Technical monospace or restrained clean sans"
      Weight: 400
      Role: "Quieter phrase within the actual quotation; verified attribution if supplied"
    Hierarchy: >
      Contrast a dominant phrase, a readable secondary register and fine graphic
      detail. Choose breaks by meaning. Leave some words quieter; do not enlarge
      every line equally. Keep all words and punctuation unambiguous.

  Layout:
    Structure:
      - "Asymmetrical modular composition"
      - "Shared alignment datums between lettering and graphic construction"
      - "Contrast between a dense assembly and an open field"
      - "Deliberate overlap, inversion and changes of scale"
      - "A clear reading sequence through the whole quotation"
    Relationships: >
      Lettering, diagrams and solid fields belong to the same architecture.
      Let graphic modules define intervals, edges or counterspaces for the text.
      A hidden grid coordinates the composition; reveal construction lines only
      where they relate to an actual element.

  Elements:
    Background:
      - "Flat paper tone"
      - "Selective solid ink fields"
    Structural:
      - "Flat geometric primitives, shared radii and sharply cut counterforms"
      - "Thin construction axes, bracket-like frames and clear terminals"
      - "Open-outline and solid versions of related planar shapes"
    Surface:
      - "Bounded parallel hatching"
      - "Geometric dot matrices"
    Images:
      - "Flat vector diagrams, shape-construction studies and typographic counterspaces"
    Selection: >
      Choose a compatible subset. Repeat a few angles, radii, line weights and
      pattern frequencies at different scales. Small marks must relate visibly
      to larger parts; a checklist of unrelated symbols does not form a system.

  Effects:
    Shadows: "None"
    Texture: "Restrained dry ink and matte paper grain"
    Depth: "Flat layering, overlap and outline-versus-solid contrast"

  Rules:
    - "Choose a visual construction idea for the quotation before adding detail."
    - "Make hierarchy and graphic relationships carry the identity."
    - "Preserve the quotation verbatim and render it once."
    - "Use only verified attribution and supplied supporting text."
    - "Keep secondary text readable at phone size."
    - "Let precision coexist with optical adjustments and expressive scale."
    - "Avoid fake data, serial numbers, certifications and filler microtext."
    - "Avoid physical product illustrations, perspective assemblies, distressed concrete, gears and hazard tape."
    - "Avoid a slogan framed by decorative bands or an unrelated emblem."
    - "Do not reproduce identities or wording from reference projects."

  User Overrides:
    Format: "Replace the ratio when requested and recompose for the new canvas."
    Palette: "Replace the colours while preserving their compositional roles."
    Fonts: >
      Honour the requested type direction; maintain hierarchy through size,
      spacing and available weight. Generated resemblance is not proof of an
      exact font match.

Card-specific art direction:
Visual idea: provisional construction marks become decisive graphic form. Develop the quotation and a family of flat geometric forms together on the page, like an experimental technical lettering specimen. Show related open-outline and filled forms moving through the same composition. These are drawn shapes and typographic counterspaces, not depictions of components or objects.

Keep every element in the plane of the paper: squares, rectangular strokes, shared semicircular counterforms, bounded hatching and thin linear connections. Their curves, spacing and proportions visibly recur in the lettering and surrounding construction. Use an orange solid plane and a few black forms to anchor the system, against an open paper field. A fine alignment line can relate two real edges; a small mark can register an actual curve. The medium-scale graphic construction must have comparable presence to the typography, with a finer subordinate register of lines and pattern.

Interleave the text and construction through shared edges and intervals. Let geometric planes shape the empty space beside and between phrases, rather than placing a separate illustration beneath a headline. Keep every letter readable and preserve a natural reading sequence. Give "non si aspetta:" a readable quieter register; contrast the scale and density of the other two phrases.

No perspective, thickness, bevels, bolts, sheet-metal plates, enclosures, machinery or product rendering. This is graphic design made with ink, typographic shapes and paper, not an illustrated hardware manual.
Text (verbatim), exactly once, and no other text:
Il futuro non si aspetta: si progetta.

Keep the original capitalization and punctuation, including the colon and final period. No attribution is available. No extra captions, labels, digits, logos or pseudo-text, including within the diagram. Every letter must be visible and readable. Deliver only the single finished flat card, without a mockup, a palette swatch panel or explanatory annotations.
```
