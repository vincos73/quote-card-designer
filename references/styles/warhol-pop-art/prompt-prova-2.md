# Warhol / Pop Art — prompt prova 2

Testo dimostrativo originale, senza attribuzione. Nuova generazione da descrizione,
senza immagini di riferimento passate al generatore.

```text
Create a bold original 4:5 vertical typographic Pop Art screenprint inspired by Andy Warhol's everyday-object photographic prints, designed as a quote poster. Flat finished artwork only, no mockup, no framing, no signature.
The ONLY written text is exactly this Italian phrase, once:
LE IDEE
FANNO
RUMORE.
Absolutely no labels, brand names, dial numbers, captions, decorative letters, author or signatures.

Make the quote the primary subject: massive heavy neo-grotesque capital typography, upright, broad and compact, carefully optically spaced. The three text lines form an assertive stack through the central and left area, occupy at least half the page visual weight and remain fully readable at small size. Black letters for LE IDEE and FANNO, warm ivory letters for RUMORE. on the deep-blue lower field. The final period is clear. The typography has subtle authentic ink texture but crisp letter shapes. Do not use comic-book lettering, speech bubbles, explosion shapes or text boxes.

Object: a distinctive old rotary telephone with curving handset and coiled cord, no branding or numbers; the circular dial is depicted purely as blank finger holes. Enormous photographic-screenprint phone rises from the lower-right corner and crops beyond the right edge, its receiver floating diagonally on the upper right attached by the coiled cord. The circular dial, cradle and receiver should be recognizable in high-contrast photographic black ink, NOT illustrative contour drawing, not 3D product rendering. Color-separated bright electric orange phone planes with saturated cyan and violet offset shadows. Intentionally imperfect registration, black thresholded photographic texture, local excess ink and occasional missing ink. One smaller repetition of the same telephone, printed as a faint magenta positive image, appears partly cropped at upper left behind the first line, a rhythmic echo rather than a regular grid. Ensure this echo does not harm text contrast.

Composition differs from a portrait poster: dynamic diagonal receiver and looping cord run through the open upper-right space, while the large phone body and type interlock in the lower half. The phone has clear recognizable negative-space silhouette and dial; words may cross quiet colored portions of the object but black details must not interfere with letters. Background consists of two large unequally sized flat ink fields, hot pink above and deep cobalt blue across lower third, with one irregular pale lemon patch behind the middle line. All share the same matte off-white fine art paper underneath. The line RUMORE. is foreground, completely unoccluded.
Strong saturation and banal everyday object elevated to an icon; deliberately flat color, dense photographic black screen, slight off-register outlines, small mesh-grain details and tactile ink. Screenprint energy rather than retro comic illustration. Do not simulate random scratch noise everywhere. No gratuitous brush scrawls, no geometric art-deco ornament, no literal torn-paper collage shadows. Original composition and ordinary unbranded object, typography dominates.
```
