# Warhol / Pop Art — prompt prova 1

Testo dimostrativo originale, senza attribuzione. Nuova generazione da descrizione,
senza immagini di riferimento passate al generatore.

```text
Create an original museum-quality contemporary quote art print inspired by Andy Warhol's photographic screenprints and 1970s portrait portfolios. Vertical 4:5 artwork, flat print full frame, no mockup, no frame, no signatures. This is a new reusable Warhol / Pop Art visual direction with typography as the primary communication.

The ONLY text in the image, exactly once, is:
CAMBIA
IL TUO
SGUARDO.
No other words, names, dates, signatures, credits or arbitrary marks that resemble writing.

The quoted phrase must be unmistakably the dominant visual element: enormous tightly composed heavyweight neo-grotesque uppercase letterforms, broad flat shapes, natural proportions, optical kerning, open counters, pristine readability, comfortably inside safe margins. Each of the three lines is large enough to read at thumbnail size. The lettering is solid black on light colored areas, with the last line in warm ivory across a dark ink field. No speech balloons, starbursts, comic lettering or decorative large polka dots.

Composition: an arresting asymmetric portrait and text weave across one shared sheet of warm off-white textured printmaking paper. A large photographed portrait of an anonymous adult woman with short dark curly hair, looking directly toward the viewer, cropped at chest, occupies primarily the right half and lower three-quarters. Her face is recognizable as a photographic face, NOT an illustration or vector drawing. A sharply thresholded BLACK photographic screen layer retains grain, eyes, lips, hair and cheek shadows; extensive white losses and irregular inking break up the dark areas subtly. Under that black photographic layer, bold non-naturalistic flat color stencils: a large dusty HOT PINK plane across cheeks, a vivid MINT GREEN irregular rectangle over one eye and temple, a muted golden ochre angular shape behind the head, a small scarlet patch on the lips. Colored stencils are intentionally slightly misregistered relative to the photographic contours. A handful of thin spontaneous black contour lines, offset from the silhouette, extend into white paper. No literal torn-paper shadows: all marks belong to the flat printed surface.
The huge phrase occupies left and center: CAMBIA across upper-left, IL TUO below, SGUARDO. across the lower-middle, interlocking with the shoulder and printed color shapes. Leave the key features of the face visible; move portrait to right if needed for readable words. Make the quote feel integrated with the color stencils and ink masses rather than placed inside a separate caption panel. Graphic balance: the words are the first read, then the face, then the print texture.
Color should feel sophisticated 1970s fine-art screenprint: warm off-white paper, black key ink, bubblegum/dusty pink, mint, ochre, small scarlet. Broad flat irregular color cutouts, photographic black shadow structure, visible squeegee drag and local ink pinholes, subtle fine photographic grain and screen texture. No uniform grunge filter, no decorative border, no glossy modern gradients, no smoothly painted or cartoonized face, no halftone comic page, no obligatory repetition grid. Preserve authentic photographic ambiguity and tactile print character.
```
