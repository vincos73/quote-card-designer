# American Comics — seconda prova

Testo dimostrativo originale: IL CORAGGIO FA STRADA.
Nuova generazione da descrizione, senza immagini passate al generatore.

```text
Create a finished original quote poster in the American Comics style, vertical 4:5, full bleed flat artwork. This is the second study of a style: vintage American adventure comic-book storytelling with a gigantic central quote exploding across the boundaries of surrounding narrative panels. Authentic bold brush and pen inking, flat four-color printing, highly visible coarse round mechanical Ben-Day halftone dots, modest plate misregistration, subtle ink breakup on fibrous warm old newsprint. Retain vibrant golden yellow, vermilion, faded cyan and warm black. No photorealism, no glossy 3D, no modern digital gradients. The paper should feel aged and handled but not overwhelmed with dirt.

ONLY TEXT anywhere on the page, exactly once:
IL CORAGGIO
FA STRADA.
This is a short original Italian demonstration phrase, no attribution. No extra captions, sound effects, signage, tiny pseudo-writing or logos.

Narrative: a courageous adult woman motorcyclist taking to an open mountain road, portrayed in authentic mid-century American adventure comic drawing. New subject matter entirely terrestrial and human: no inventor, laboratory, rocket or futuristic city. The same protagonist has a short dark bob, a cream open-face helmet with riding goggles, a red scarf, a dark leather jacket and brown gloves, consistent across panels. Motorbike classic 1950s road bike with large round headlamp, no branding.
Compose five asymmetrical narrative panels with cream gutters and black irregular ink outlines, readable around the large central quote:
- upper wide panel: closeup three-quarter view of the rider's determined face, goggles raised, scarf whipping, blue sky with halftone shading;
- narrow left-middle panel: a single anatomically correct gloved hand gripping the handlebar throttle and brake, dynamic closeup;
- upper-right/side panel: a winding switchback road through a mountain pass with pine silhouettes, no letters on any scenery;
- lower-left panel: front three-quarter action view of the rider and motorcycle leaning naturally into a curve, road dust and strong inked motion lines;
- lower-right panel: a broad vista of a stone bridge leading between cliffs toward the horizon, warm evening light.
Use varied shot scales and a coherent continuous journey. Panel divisions may be angled subtly, unlike a rigid equal grid.

Quote design: an irregular jagged pale cream explosion with golden yellow inner rays and vermilion edge spikes fills the center, breaks across the ink panel boundaries and projects outward into the scenes. Only two main lines, gently rising with energetic comic lettering: 'IL CORAGGIO' then 'FA STRADA.'. Massive naturally condensed hand-drawn display capitals with solid black contours, a restrained red offset shadow and visible halftone inside the letters. First line golden yellow, second line vermilion with a narrow cream inline highlight. Lettering is dominant, beautiful and unmistakably legible, not bent or squeezed. Open counters, optical spacing, one visible final period. The quote occupies the middle 40-45 percent of the page height, giving the story panels more room than a dense full-page explosion. Keep quote centered and safe from cropping, all words visible. The explosion is an organic part of the comics page rather than a rectangular overlay. Keep mechanical dot texture particularly legible in skin, sky and letter fills; fiber/grain is secondary to the regular dots. Strong narrative personality and attractive crafted illustration.
```
