# American Comics — prima prova

Testo dimostrativo originale scelto per lo studio, senza attribuzione.
Reference dell'utente osservata per direzione visiva; non passata come asset al generatore.

```text
Create one exceptional finished quote poster that defines an original reusable design style called American Comics. Vertical 4:5 composition, flat artwork edge to edge, no mockup.
The ONLY written words in the entire image are this brief Italian demonstration quote, exactly:
IL FUTURO
NON ASPETTA.
INVENTALO.
No author, no title, no labels, no speech text, no sound-effect words, no additional letters.

Creative direction: authentic vintage American adventure comic book page, mid-century inexpensive four-color letterpress/offset printing on warm aged newsprint. Highly visible mechanically regular round halftone dots, coarse Ben-Day screen, flat limited ink colors, slight plate misregistration at colored contours, subtle ink breakup and paper fibers. Golden yellow, vermilion red, slightly faded cyan blue, heavy warm black, cream paper. The page must still feel colorful, forceful and beautifully drawn, only moderately aged, not brown or heavily distressed.

Build a real story page behind the central quotation: 5-6 cropped asymmetric rectangular panels separated by narrow cream gutters and hand-inked black borders. Rich confidently drawn original adventure scenes suggesting inventing tomorrow: a woman inventor in rolled-up sleeves at a glowing mechanism top left, a dramatic closeup eye and brow top right, a hand operating a lever on one side, a retro-futurist rocket launch lower left, a futuristic city lower right. Characters and anatomy drawn with economical mid-century brush inking, hard graphic shadows and halftone color fields. No recognizable franchise characters. Panels remain identifiable and occupy the entire background, with narrative fragments clearly visible along all four edges.

The quote bursts out from the middle and breaks across several panel boundaries. An enormous irregular jagged cream-and-yellow explosion shape behind the lettering with fine red/black energy spikes projecting into neighboring panels. All three parts of the quote read instantly and in correct order. 'IL FUTURO' on the upper central line, 'NON ASPETTA.' below, 'INVENTALO.' on the final emphatic line. The central type should cover roughly 65 percent of the width and 50 percent of height; the final line may be wider. Bold custom-drawn American comic display lettering, naturally mildly forward leaning, solid black outline, narrow red offset dimensional shadow. Golden yellow fills for first two lines, red vermilion with cream inset for the last line, subtle visible halftone on the letter fills. Optical spacing, natural letter shapes, open counters, perfectly readable punctuation. No warped text, no words cut off. Explosion and lettering are organically part of the page and partially overlap the comic artwork, not an ordinary rectangular text card pasted on top.
Use the aesthetic described here to create a newly designed coherent visual, not a reproduction of a particular existing cover. The quotation must clearly dominate while the comic panels retain personality and narrative detail.
```
