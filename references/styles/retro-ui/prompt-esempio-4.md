```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one complete 4:5 vertical quote card, flat full-bleed artwork, no mockup or photographed screen.
Create a refined pastel retro desktop with early bitmap GUI details, reinterpreted as a contemporary quote card. The material is matte digital print, not shiny glass. Use pale mint/aqua (#BFD9D1) and warm cream (#F3EAD4), dark charcoal contours (#202421), a muted coral selection accent (#CE816D), and a small muted teal field (#6BADA8). Broad curving cream/mint wallpaper bands sweep through the desktop background. Every interface frame, pictogram and letter shares a crisp, low-resolution stepped edge language, with thin well-spaced borders and only a small hard offset shadow.
Composition: an active wide document window occupies the upper and middle right, with its top edge set below a thin desktop menu band. Within this large cream document panel, the quote appears huge and left aligned, with a comfortable margin and plenty of breathing room. Its bottom edge is above the bottom fifth of the card. A smaller picture-preview window overlaps only its empty lower left margin and extends toward the lower left desktop. That preview contains a simple original black/teal bitmap illustration of a sprout emerging from an open folder; no photo and no photorealistic detail. A slim vertical icon-only tool palette peeks from the far left. In the quiet lower right desktop gap there is a single folded-page icon with a small selection rectangle. These three unequal window scales create an asymmetrical but calm workspace, rather than a mechanically centered card inside two identical rectangles. The text always sits in front and is never occluded. The major quotation panel has a striped header interrupted by a small empty title inset, a small square close box on the left, and an authentic narrow scrollbar on the right. No macOS traffic-light controls, no thick neo-brutalist outlines.
Text, verbatim, once only: "FAI SPAZIO A CIÒ CHE CONTA."
Typeset as a bold and very legible bitmap-derived proportional system font, not a default slab-serif monospace and not a modern smooth geometric sans. Keep a common letter design and optical weight. Suggested three lines:
FAI SPAZIO
A CIÒ CHE
CONTA.
The accented Ò in CIÒ and the final period must be exact. Natural letter spacing and word spacing; no letter deformation.
The quotation is the only prose in the image; no extra labels, filenames, menu words, dates, times, software names, music lyrics, social handles, logos, watermark or attribution. The thin top desktop band uses only small abstract menu pictograms, not invented text. Avoid repeated miscellaneous icons, illustration as a tiny disconnected sticker, giant black shadow slabs, paper aging, chrome, glassmorphism, rainbow gradients, and a central blank ivory panel floating alone. Give the workspace mature visual rhythm, the quote first and the small sprout illustration second.
```
