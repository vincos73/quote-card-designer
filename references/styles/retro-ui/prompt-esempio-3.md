```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a finished single 4:5 vertical quote card, flat artwork filling the image, no photographed monitor and no mockup.
Create a strong, carefully crafted retro graphical desktop composition using black and white only. It should feel like a mid-1980s monochrome bitmap desktop reimagined as a contemporary quote card: crisp one-bit graphics, a shared square-pixel logic, fine orderly checkerboard dithering, thin double rules, striped active title bars, restrained white outlined desktop folder icons, proper scroll arrows and size boxes. It must have richer interface character than two generic empty rectangles with drop shadows.
Composition: solid black desktop visible between four unequal windows. A large active white notepad window sits low and left, occupying most of the lower two thirds and almost the full usable width; its pinstriped title bar and slim vertical scrollbar are unmistakably classic bitmap GUI. Give its content generous insets. This notepad carries the whole dominant quote, black heavy bitmap system lettering, easily readable even in a small feed. Behind its upper edge, a medium image-preview window on the upper right shows one large pixel-art crescent moon and tiny clouds against a precisely dithered field. A short narrow utility strip at upper left contains only simple playback pictograms and one slider. One small white outline folder icon and one selected icon outline live in a quiet black desktop gap. A small white arrow cursor sits by the notepad's empty checkbox control near the bottom, never over a letter. Windows overlap with clear front/back hierarchy; secondary panels never hide the quote. Most of the text area stays white, with no pattern behind the letters.
Text, verbatim, once only: "NON DEVI ESSERE SEMPRE DISPONIBILE."
Preferred line groups:
NON DEVI
ESSERE
SEMPRE
DISPONIBILE.
All four lines belong to the same substantial bitmap lettering register; natural word gaps, coherent pixel steps and widths, no stretched or squashed letters. The final period must remain. Ensure the final long word fits with natural proportions.
Pure black and white is the intended palette; gray may only be the optical result of tiny ordered black/white dithering and raster edge smoothing. No gradients, no colored accents, no glow, no chrome, no grain filter pretending to be pixels.
The quote is the only prose anywhere. Keep title bars unlabeled using stripes, and all controls use symbols only. No application names, song titles, dates, clocks, numbers, URLs, logos, brand icons, fake microcopy, author name, watermark, decorative stars, faces or humans. Deliver a convincing complete quote card, not a screenshot collage inventory.
```
