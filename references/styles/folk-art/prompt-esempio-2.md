# Prompt effettivo · Folk Art · gesto di cura

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one complete, finished vertical 4:5 editorial quote card, full bleed, flat artwork seen straight on, not a photograph of a print. Compose the lettering and painted botanical ornament as one work.

An original contemporary folk-art painting with a warm ivory paper field. Matte hand-painted pigment, precise but human contours, fine visible brush strokes, selective small hatch and dot details inside broad leaves and layered flower petals. Restrained palette of dark evergreen, deep burgundy, dusty coral rose and mustard gold on warm ivory. No blue in this application. Keep the surface clean, with subtle uncoated-paper grain and very light pigment variation, rather than stained or distressed antique paper.

The main phrase must dominate immediately in a single uninterrupted top-to-bottom reading route in the upper and central-left part of the card. Large, robust Western painted serif capitals, slightly human edges, open counters, coherent width and natural word spaces. Use dark evergreen for most lettering and burgundy for one meaningful line if useful. No blackletter, no western saloon lettering, no tiny connective words. Exact only text, once:
"OGNI GESTO DI CURA FA FIORIRE IL MONDO."
Use these clearly aligned groups:
OGNI GESTO
DI CURA
FA FIORIRE
IL MONDO.
The final full stop must be visible. Keep all words the same substantial readable register; do not place them in scattered pockets or curved paths.

A single expressive flowering tree rises from the lower third: a broad, slim decorative trunk and curling branches spread mainly toward the right side and around the lower edge of the quotation. The trunk and boughs are dark evergreen; layered burgundy, ochre and dusty rose blossoms are richly detailed at close inspection. One large flower anchors the open upper-right area, balancing the sentence, while smaller buds lead the eye down toward branching roots at the foot of the card. Allow small paired leaves and petal echoes to create a rhythm of near-symmetry locally, but make the overall composition asymmetrical and spacious. The open silhouette of the boughs creates a continuous cream area for the entire text block. This is not a wreath, not a uniform pattern, not a central animal poster, and not a box of text pasted onto an illustration. Keep all stems and petals away from the essential letter strokes. A few small painted seed dots and a simple broken botanical perimeter are enough; do not fill every gap.

Folk-art affinities should be expressed through the painted decorative grammar, stylised layered plant forms, selective internal marks and a patient crafted rhythm. Do not present the work as a replica of any particular tradition. No birds, hearts, cat, sun or moon are required in this application. No additional wording, author, signature, logo, watermark, dates, numbers, labels, pseudo text, frame mockup, photographic lighting, three-dimensional shadows, glossy plastic, photorealistic flowers, heavy grunge, fake torn-paper collage, technological symbols, or four-point starbursts.
