# Prompt effettivo · Folk Art · radici e volo

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one complete, finished vertical 4:5 editorial quote card, full bleed, flat artwork seen straight on, not a photo of a framed print. Design illustration and lettering together.

Contemporary painted folk-art language: a warm uncoated cream paper field, rich matte opaque pigments, confident handmade silhouettes with fine brush marks and small variations in edge and paint coverage. A lively border of curling leafy stems, layered tulip-like and rosette flowers, tiny berries, and restrained seed-shaped marks. Use an approximately bilateral balance with subtle differences between the sides, not a perfectly mirrored digital vector. Deep indigo blue is the dominant lettering and outline pigment, with forest green, brick red, warm ochre and cream. Keep the number of colours controlled. The fine paper grain and light pigment wear should be tactile but the paper remains clean and readable.

Make the quotation the first thing to read. Large, substantial Western painted serif capitals with natural, slightly human contours, clear counters, strong contrast, natural word spacing and generous line separation; no blackletter, no circus lettering. The exact complete quotation is the only text in the image, rendered once:
"LE RADICI CUSTODISCONO. I SOGNI FANNO VOLARE."
Suggested clear reading groups in a broad, open cream field:
LE RADICI
CUSTODISCONO.
I SOGNI
FANNO VOLARE.
Keep both full stops clearly visible. Preserve every word and natural glyph proportions; do not compress the long word to fit.

Two medium-sized stylised birds, one on either side of a flowering branch below and partly alongside the quotation, enrich the visual meaning of roots and flight. Their silhouettes are related, but wing markings, head angle and painted details differ. Ornament grows out of a branching base, turns around the sentence and completes a slender painted scalloped perimeter; the cream void around the text feels shaped by the foliage. Small hand-painted petal details within the bird wings connect the birds to the frame. The letters remain whole and unobscured. The birds and flowers are purposeful framing masses, with quiet cream intervals close to the words. Ornamental detail can be denser toward the perimeter. No giant isolated heart, no cottage, no landscape panel.

Avoid copied traditional artefacts or identifiable reference compositions. This is an original contemporary quote card with folk-art affinities, not an antique certificate or ethnographic facsimile. No additional wording, author, signature, logo, watermark, dates, numbers, labels, pseudo text, mockup, three-dimensional shadows, gloss, photorealism, cute 3D animals, heavy grunge, fake torn-paper collage, technological symbols, or four-point starbursts.
