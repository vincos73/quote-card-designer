# Prompt dell’esempio

Composizione dimostrativa: il soggetto e le parole appartengono a questa card.
Non usare questo prompt come impaginato fisso per nuove citazioni.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one finished artistic social quote card, portrait 4:5, flat artwork filling the canvas.

Design style: Dada Black. Create an expressive, experimental editorial photomontage inspired by Dada and vintage handmade collage. Torn paper, magazine-cut typography, archival-looking photographic cutouts and hand-drawn illustration are the actual medium. Make the collage feel authored through scale, unexpected juxtaposition, irregular edges and purposeful empty space.

Visual idea: an incomplete anonymous vintage black-and-white portrait is being constructed by an oversized photographic cutout of a hand drawing its missing contour. The torn photographic edge continues into a displaced, unfinished pencil outline on another layer of paper. Treat this as one surprising, physically impossible photomontage expressing active creation, with a little visual wit. Keep the fragments visibly cut and assembled; do not paint a seamless surreal scene. This single image relationship carries the story.

Canvas and palette: deep dark olive #2E3122 as the dominant background. Off-white #F4F4F4 type and beige #D9CDB2 paper. Select accents sparingly from muted olive #788B5A, purple #8A6283, blue #74879A and terracotta #B55252; the palette need not use every accent. Matte paper grain, varied natural torn edges and restrained soft contact shadows only where paper layers overlap.

Composition: asymmetrical editorial layout with collage layering and torn-paper framing. Balance a concentrated zone of photographic fragments with a quieter open field. Give the quotation a clear, immediate reading sequence and ample scale for a phone screen. Let a few paper edges overlap and some fragments sit at different angles, while keeping all words readable. Avoid a central rectangular quote panel. The lettering and photomontage should share the composition rather than sit in separate text/image compartments.

Typography: magazine-cutout typography is the expressive voice. Build a few coherent word or phrase clippings with contrasting size, weight and printed character; do not assign a random font to every letter. Use thin clean sans-serif lettering as a secondary register for quieter parts of the same quotation. Use expressive cutout treatment especially for "futuro" and "progetta." Preserve the natural Italian reading order, the original capitalization, every word and all punctuation. No text may be hidden by collage layers or cropped by the canvas.

Render this exact quotation ONCE, and no other text:
Il futuro non si aspetta: si progetta.

Constraints: no attribution because the author is unverified. No invented labels, captions, dates, signatures, logos, pseudo-newspaper text or watermark. Paper fragments may have vintage label shapes but must contain no extra lettering. No need to include every decorative element from the style vocabulary: botanical drawings and additional portraits are optional and should be omitted unless necessary for this concept. No generic scrapbook arrangement, evenly distributed decorative scraps, uniform torn-edge filter, glossy digital effects, sterile corporate slide, or neo-industrial grid. Deliver only the single finished card, not a mockup or a presentation board.
```
