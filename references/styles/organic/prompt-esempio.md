Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one complete flat editorial quote card, portrait 4:5, full bleed, not a framed object or a wall mockup.
Primary request: create an original organic graphic quote card. A strong readable Italian sentence and a few large simplified botanical shapes must share one carefully composed page.
Style and material: opaque flat cut-paper colour silhouettes translated into a tactile screenprinted poster. Bold irregular flower petals, broad wavy leaves and a curved stem; contours feel confidently hand cut, smooth but not mechanically perfect. A few thin negative-space veins describe the leaves. Very light paper fibre and sparse tiny ink skips inside the colour; generous clean cream negative space, no heavy distress, no realistic volume or cast shadows.
Composition: a large confident dark-forest-green grotesk sentence occupies the upper-left and middle-left field in four clear left-aligned lines. The line breaks are OGNI COSA / CRESCE / AL PROPRIO / RITMO. CRESCE may be slightly more emphatic, but keep one coherent bold sans design, natural glyph proportions and clear word spaces. A lively oversized asymmetrical orange flower opens from the lower-right area; its curved green stem and two broad leaves rise from the lower edge. The uppermost petal and one leaf approach the open right side of the words and optically answer the type block, without touching or covering letters. Use a few large forms with an asymmetrical diagonal rising rhythm. Some botanical forms can crop at the lower or right boundary. Preserve a real quiet interval between the words and foliage. This is a quote card with expressive visual mass, not a specimen sheet, repeated pattern, bouquet illustration, symmetrical floral wreath or centered inspirational template.
Palette chosen for this study: warm cream paper #F3EEDC, deep forest green #17483D for all the text and much of the foliage, warm orange #EF7B33 for the principal flower, a very small ochre #D9AD3F detail. Solid matte inks; no gradient.
Text verbatim, exactly once, including the final period: "OGNI COSA CRESCE AL PROPRIO RITMO."
Content constraints: all and only those words, correctly spelled and in uppercase. No attribution or signature. Every word and the period must be fully visible, high contrast, comfortably legible at phone scale. The sentence leads the reading order; illustration supports its rhythm. Do not stretch a word to fill a line or reduce connecting words into tiny captions.
Avoid: brand patterns or recognizable copied flowers, extra slogans, event details, labels, dates, logos, watermarks, numbers, decorative microtext, decorative line frames, four-point stars, detailed realistic botanical drawing, photographs, watercolor transparency, 3D, glossy texture, shadows, crumpled paper, dense folk ornament, grunge and random speckles across the type.
