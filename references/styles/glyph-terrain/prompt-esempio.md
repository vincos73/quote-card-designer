# Prompt effettivo, esempio 1

Input visivi: le tre immagini Atlas fornite dall’utente, passate a ImageGen come reference.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: finished 4:5 vertical editorial quote card
Primary request: Create a bold experimental graphic poster in the Glyph Terrain style. Design an asymmetric map of three or four large irregular FLAT color territories with fine stepped seams, overprinted by many genuinely tiny numerals and punctuation-like marks. The microtype changes density across seams and thins toward a generous quiet opening. The territories, seam and microtype remain part of one integrated composition beside the quote.
Composition family: seam-clearing. Place the large quotation in the quiet opening, with color territories approaching around its sides; typography is the first thing read at thumbnail size. Do not place all texture in detached top or bottom bands.
Typography: clear geometric sans serif, clean natural glyphs, medium or semibold weight, large and linear, carefully spaced; use 2 or 3 natural lines. Quote ink is dark evergreen and has high contrast. The quotation is NOT made of digits. No other readable text.
Color palette (chosen independently from all references): warm ivory #EFE9DA as calm ground; dark evergreen #173B37 for quotation; muted terracotta #B96652, dusty lilac #8580A7 and pale celadon #B9CDB0 as flat territory inks. Microtype in evergreen and occasional ivory, adjusted for contrast. Matte print surface with restrained grain.
Text (verbatim, exactly once): “Ogni scelta dà forma al futuro.”
Spelling: O-g-n-i / s-c-e-l-t-a / d-à / f-o-r-m-a / a-l / f-u-t-u-r-o. Include final period.
Constraints: microtype is tiny, no larger than one quarter of the quote capital height; quote remains dominant and clear; preserve every accent and punctuation mark; image must look like a finished poster rather than a mockup.
Avoid: copying an existing silhouette, palette or number sequence; giant pixel blocks, generic glitch, gradients, 3D effects, data legends, labels, attribution, logo, watermark.
Input images: All three attached Atlas images are reference studies only. Transfer their relationship between irregular flat color zones, fine stepped borders and dense typographic microtexture. Do not reuse their palettes, particular boundaries, number strings, or square layout.
```
