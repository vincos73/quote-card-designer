# Retro-futurism Art · revisione psichedelica

Prompt effettivo per ImageGen integrato, card completa, senza immagini di input.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one complete flat portrait 4:5 editorial quote card, full bleed, straight-on artwork.
Create an original psychedelic retro-futurist cosmic photomontage, richly strange and tactile like a heavily printed experimental speculative paperback from the early 1970s. It must combine a massive human-scale fragment, celestial inner space, colour-layered contours and a mineral earthly ground, not a neat landscape with a planet.
Main composition: one monumental anonymous human hand rises obliquely from the lower-right mineral ground toward the top-centre. Show a coherent relaxed open hand silhouette with exactly five fingers, slightly bent in an expressive reaching gesture, not holding an object. The hand itself is a cutout window filled with dense indigo-black space, tiny stars and a dusty magenta nebula; there is no normal skin shading inside it. Two strongly offset contour echoes, one warm yellow and one cyan-blue, run behind parts of the hand and separate into broad irregular colour strata like old optical colour separations. Their drift should suggest an expanded inner cosmos. They are solid flat printed strata, not a modern soft glowing aura. The dark cosmic palm and fingers remain recognisable.
An abruptly cropped mineral plateau in burnt sienna and faded lilac spreads across the lower quarter. Let one fragment of terrestrial cloud or rock appear inside the cosmic hand at an incompatible smaller scale, as if the hand contains another world. The background is deep dusty plum and near-black with a selective spread of small stars and rough print grain. The montage has graphic discontinuities, overlapping cut edges and unnatural scale relationships; it should be compelling at a glance and rich in material detail, never glossy or conventionally scenic.
Typography: large, soft-shouldered expanded 1970s editorial grotesk with substantial rounded strokes, a distinct Western word shape and open counters. Warm pale yellow letters are printed directly into the dark collage. The full quote reads in four bold groups in the broad left field and lower-middle open interval: ANCHE / L'IMPOSSIBILE / HA UN / ORIZZONTE. Let the large word ORIZZONTE. span beneath the palm in the lower-middle area, while the upper groups follow two coherent left axes. Keep a simple recoverable top-to-bottom route and every word large enough to read in a phone feed. The hand and offset strata may approach the word shapes and guide their placement but must not cover a letter, apostrophe or final full stop. No separate text panel and no little subtitle. Do not stretch or squeeze one word to avoid the hand.
Materials: real-looking old colour-separated print, dense grain, visible irregular round halftone, small misregistration at silhouette edges and dusty matte paper. Keep the main lettering substantially intact. Strong cyan, weathered magenta, indigo-black, ochre-yellow and burnt-earth relations; use colour boldly with actual dark-light contrast. No chrome, HDR light, glass or 3D render.
Text verbatim exactly once: "ANCHE L'IMPOSSIBILE HA UN ORIZZONTE."
Preserve all words, apostrophe, uppercase, word spaces and the final period. No author or signature. No other text, credits, microcopy, logos, labels, coordinates or numbers.
Avoid: copying a known cosmic profile or paperback image, identifiable person, astronaut, cyborg, rocket, CRT computer, rainbow as a literal preset, tourist poster composition, generic clean vector illustration, neon/synthwave grids, glossy digital concept art, extra fingers, disconnected fingers, multiple hands, miniature connecting words, decorative four-point starbursts, watermark.
