# Retro-futurism Art · revisione psichedelica

Prompt effettivo per ImageGen integrato, card completa, senza immagini di input.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one complete flat portrait 4:5 quote card, full bleed, viewed straight on.
Create an original densely printed psychedelic cosmic collage with the force of an experimental late-1960s/1970s science-fiction magazine cover. The scene should feel strange, boldly assembled and materially printed, with incompatible scales brought into a convincing graphic relationship.
Main collage: a gigantic tilted circular aperture fills the right two-thirds and crops at the right edge. Within its cut edge is a photographic-looking turquoise mountain world upside down, with its ridges hanging downward into a dark star-filled opening. Outside the aperture, coral-red and burnt-ochre salt ridges rise in conflicting scale. A huge unbranded ivory magnetic tape reel sits half-buried near the lower-left foreground; its dark tape unspools, curves upward and becomes a thin inked orbital ribbon that passes through the aperture. The reel is an analogue object, not a spacecraft or a computer. Keep the tape reel simple, mechanically recognisable, without words or numbers. Use a few distant spectral fragments or tiny moons only if they reinforce the scale inversion.
Graphic character: assertive photographic cutout edges, flat false-colour terrain, density-rich colour separations, dusty matte stock, visible coarse round-dot halftone, dense fine grain, selective registration shifts at cyan/coral boundaries, and patches of ink dropout. The collage should have an uncanny, saturated, gritty physical presence rather than polished digital concept-art depth. Do not make a clean tourism poster. Preserve an authored hierarchy among fragments, with several different scales and material contrasts.
Typography is part of this physical printed collage and is a primary message. Use substantial expanded curvilinear 1970s Western display lettering, broad shoulders and soft heavy curves, natural legible glyphs and generous counters. Print warm ivory with a tight, very restrained coral shadow/offset along outer edges; the main letter silhouettes stay crisp. Position the complete sentence in three large semantic groups descending from upper-left into the open dark area between the aperture and foreground: IL FUTURO COMINCIA / OLTRE CIÒ CHE / CONOSCIAMO. The first line can sit on a shallow rising arc, while the next groups step lower on a related left anchor. All words remain substantial. The text is not in a separate label, plain rectangular header band or quiet caption. Shape the collage density around the letters, with high local contrast and clear full word spaces, rather than hiding letters.
Palette chosen for this study: dark ink/navy cosmic areas, intense weathered cyan and turquoise, dusty coral red, burnt ochre, small warm cream highlights. Thick matte inks and altered colour; the palette is limited but forceful, not pastel and not neon bloom.
Text verbatim, exactly once, including the grave accent and final period: "IL FUTURO COMINCIA OLTRE CIÒ CHE CONOSCIAMO."
CIÒ has a grave accent. Every word, accent and full stop must be present, correctly ordered, fully visible and readable at phone size. No author, personal signature or additional copy.
Avoid: recognizable existing movie or book cover composition, monolith, copied rainbow-head silhouette, people or astronauts by default, tourist destination branding, labels, fake coordinates, credits, author names, logos, numbers, watermarks, HUD panels, synthwave grid, clean vector scenery, glossy 3D, random stock collage, distortion or texture that erases letter shapes, tiny connecting words, four-point decorative starbursts.
