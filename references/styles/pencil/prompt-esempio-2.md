# Prompt effettivo · Origami in grafite

## Generazione iniziale

Create ONE new vertical 4:5 Italian editorial quote card in the experimental style 'Pencil'. Make a clearly DIFFERENT composition from a large quote at upper left with bowl and pebble at lower right. Exact quotation to display ONCE, with no added text, quote marks, signature or attribution: «Le idee prendono forma un tratto alla volta.» Preserve spelling and final period. Prefer three semantic lines: 'Le idee prendono' / 'forma un tratto' / 'alla volta.' Put the quotation in a generous quiet field in the LOWER RIGHT or lower central area, in a readable literary serif made of dark dry graphite #292A29. Keep letter proportions and natural kerning; no artificial tracking, stretching or fade that harms reading. Near the UPPER LEFT, always include one SMALL recognisable hand-drawn pencil illustration: a simple partly folded paper bird in progress, with its final contour emerging from pale construction lines. The illustration is smaller than the text block and not a photograph or mockup. Make the pencil consistencies visibly varied: very light 2H planning strokes and faint geometric fold guides, medium HB contours, several weighty 4B/6B strokes in the wing and underside, directional hatching, a restrained soft graphite smudge for shadow, a few erased lifts where paper catches light. Let the dry graphite catch the fine tooth of warm matte paper #F2F0E9. More tonal contrast and heavier marks than the first Pencil trial, but keep the main quote dark, clear and dominant at phone size. Give the drawing and text a shared diagonal rhythm without a box or border. Leave broad untouched paper around both. Monochrome graphite only. Avoid wet ink bleeding, ink splashes, red color, watercolor, charcoal blocks, generic noise, random doodles, fake handwritten notes, pages, dates, logo, pencil object, desk, sketchbook photo, 3D or extra words.
