# Prompt effettivo · Natura morta

Create ONE complete vertical 4:5 Italian editorial quote card in a new style called 'Pencil'. It is a flat finished artwork, not a photograph of a sketchbook. Exact text to display ONCE, preserving both accents and the final period, with no added quotation marks or attribution: «Anche ciò che lasciamo fuori cambia il senso di ciò che resta.» Typeset in four meaningful lines: 'Anche ciò che' / 'lasciamo fuori' / 'cambia il senso' / 'di ciò che resta.' Place this block in the upper-left to central area, spacious and dominant, in an elegant readable literary serif rendered in dark graphite #292A29 with a very subtle dry pencil grain at the edges. Normal glyph proportions, natural word spacing and optical kerning, no stretched letters or arbitrary tracking. Text must remain clearly legible on a phone. Warm matte off-white paper #F2F0E9 with delicate tooth and broad untouched areas. ALWAYS include a small recognisable pencil image: in the lower-right, draw a small shallow ceramic bowl with one pebble resting outside it, as a quiet visual relation between inside and outside. This is an authored graphite drawing, not a photo or icon. Keep it decisively smaller than the quotation. Show distinct graphite consistencies within the drawing: very light 2H/HB exploratory construction lines, medium-weight contour, concentrated soft 4B/6B dark strokes at the rim and pebble, directional hatching and crosshatching for volume, a little rubbed graphite shadow and small eraser-lifted highlights. Some construction lines may remain visible, but the image must read clearly at card size. Let the pencil marks reveal paper tooth; use dry graphite texture, NO wet ink bleeding, splashes, red accents, watercolor, charcoal blocks or uniform distressed overlay. No frame, margin rule, landscape, plant, handwriting, labels, date, page number, signature, logo, mockup, pencil object, desk, extra text or decorative filler. The quotation and the small drawing should feel designed together through their spacing and tonal balance.
