# Esempio 2: miniatura marginale

Prompt effettivo usato per la prima versione della variante:

> Create ONE finished 4:5 vertical Italian quote card in the reusable Illuminated Folio visual language, as a DIFFERENT composition from a left-vine illuminated-O card. The exact sentence must appear ONCE, large and as the first thing read: “Una buona domanda sposta il confine.” Set the complete quote in one readable dark-brown bookhand-inspired serif, sentence case, natural spacing, four balanced lines in a generous central writing field. Do not replace or repeat its first letter with a decorated initial in this example. Build a richly painted but asymmetrical medieval folio around the text: a delicate top band of red, dusty rose and olive acanthus; on the right margin, winding stems, seedpods and a tiny fantastical bird; near the lower-left edge, one small framed miniature of a traveler facing an open gate, drawn with flat tempera pigment and fine quill outlines. Let one tendril trace the quote block's edge without touching its letters. A few restrained flat-gold details, pale parchment fibre, softly worn pigment. Leave enough clear parchment behind ALL quote words; the ornamental zones are lively but subordinate. Avoid a complete symmetrical floral box frame. Make it feel painted on one page, not collaged from several sources. Do not include any other writing, pseudo-Latin, filler calligraphy, musical notes, attribution, title, label, date, page number, coat of arms, saint, book mockup, shiny gold or 3D effects. Preserve the exact accent and final period.
