# Esempio 1: capolettera

Prompt effettivo usato per generare `esempio.png`:

> Create ONE finished 4:5 vertical Italian quote card in a new medieval illuminated manuscript style called Illuminated Folio. Treat the attached images as visual references for materials and the relationship among decorated initial, painted marginalia, parchment and writing; do not copy a page layout, person, emblem, manuscript text or exact scene. The quotation must be the PRIMARY visual message and large enough to read easily on a phone. Exact Italian quotation ONCE: “Ogni scelta dà forma al futuro.” Compose it across three or four generous lines in dark oak-brown ink, using one readable humanist bookhand-inspired serif with natural kerning and word spaces. Make the first letter O an oversized illuminated initial that is the actual first character of “Ogni”, not an extra duplicate. Inside the O, paint a tiny branching sapling, simple and symbolic; the rest of the first word “gni” starts immediately after the initial, followed by the remaining exact words in normal reading order. If the decorated initial risks text accuracy, instead set the full sentence plainly and use a non-letter miniature alongside it. Build an asymmetrical folio: climbing blue and green tendrils along the LEFT margin, a few small vermilion flowers and one tiny bird near the upper edge, sparse flat gold pinpoints, broad quiet parchment around the large quote. One edge may be cropped; do not make a uniform box frame. Fine quill contours, matte pigments, subtle parchment fibres, flat muted gilding. The text and painting share an alignment and feel made on the same page. No small pseudo-Latin body copy, no repeated quotation, no invented author, labels, folio number, heraldry, religious scene, seal, realistic book mockup, glossy gold or extra text.
