# Prova 2 · Dawn Bloom, formato quadrato

Prompt effettivo per ImageGen integrato. Nessuna immagine di riferimento inviata al generatore; prova del profilo testuale.

Alla prima versione segue una [correzione mirata dello spazio fra parole](prompt-correzione-2.md). La prima immagine è conservata in `prova-2-originale.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE finished Italian editorial quote card, true 1:1 square canvas, flat full bleed.

PRIMARY REQUEST: Test the self-contained Prismatic Flutes design language with a longer quotation, a luminous palette and a square format instead of the portrait default. Compose the optical field and typography together as a complete, resolved editorial artwork. No input reference images.

VISUAL CONSTRUCTION: A continuous atmospheric field of broad, strongly defocused lilac, rose and peach light appears through many shallow vertical optical flutes. Approximately 40–55 slender equal-pitch flutes across the whole width. Each one reveals a slightly displaced and gently light-to-dark modulated slice of the SAME underlying field, with a narrow tonal reset at its edge. Their axes stay vertical while the colour pressure moves obliquely. Read as luminous reeded glass in abstraction, not opaque alternating stripes, physical rods or a photograph of glass.

PALETTE FOR THIS TEST: Pale lavender #E6DFF4 and lilac #CBC6ED in the upper and left areas, soft rose #EA8FAF across a broad diagonal passage, with a diffuse apricot-peach bloom (#F8CD88, #FFB779) emerging near the lower-right and beyond the crop. Keep the light broad, unequal and optically defocused; there are no outlined circles or sharply recognisable objects. The reading field is luminous and sufficiently even for deep-plum ink #262037 throughout every word.

SURFACE: Fine dry monochromatic grain covers the colour field, including its light zones, at a much smaller scale than the flute pitch. Keep broad soft colour, narrower shallow flute transitions and fine grain at distinct scales. Delicate tactile material, not distressed paper, glossy plastic or deep extruded ribs.

COMPOSITION: Recompose specifically for the square, not a crop of a portrait card. The large left-aligned text block occupies the lower-middle left and extends confidently toward the centre-right. Leave a visibly breathing luminous field above, with the warm bloom pressing toward the block from the lower-right. Keep the actual word area in a coherent pale tonal region, with the same subtle fluting continuing behind it. Meaningful margins, roughly 10 percent; no separate text panel or box. The complete quotation is the primary reading mass, with a visibly different light/text relationship from a quote placed above a lower-left glow.

TEXT (verbatim, display exactly once on these four lines):
Cambiare prospettiva
è il primo passo
per vedere
possibilità nuove.

TYPOGRAPHY: A crisp contemporary neo-grotesque sans serif of medium weight, visually in the direction of Inter Medium. Natural glyph widths, open counters, optical kerning and ordinary word spacing, compact but readable leading. One consistent size and weight throughout the four lines, dark plum #262037. Keep the first line large enough to establish a substantial editorial text block; do not shrink the quotation into a small caption. Preserve the grave accents in è and possibilità and the final period. No artificial compression or stretched words to make lines equal in width. Letterforms are clean and sharp IN FRONT OF the refraction; no blur, glow, slicing, grain erosion or duplicate text.

CONSTRAINTS: Only the supplied quotation appears. No author, personal signature, heading, labels, logo, microtext, quotation marks or decorative icons. No mockup, photographed print, frame, horizontal bands, hard rainbow gradient stops, embossed letters, thick bevels, metal curtains or glossy tubes. Deliver one complete flat square card, not a multi-panel comparison.
```
